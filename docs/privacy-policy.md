## 1. Who we are

KeptCare is a patient engagement and retention platform for medical practices, operated by
KeptCare (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in the Republic of South Africa ("KeptCare", "we",
"us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal
information, and the rights you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of
2013 ("POPIA").

## 2. Our two roles under POPIA

KeptCare handles personal information in two different capacities:

- **As a responsible party** for information about our own customers and website visitors:
  the practices that subscribe to KeptCare, their staff users, people who request a demo, and
  visitors to our website. We decide why and how this information is processed.
- **As an operator** for patient information. Each subscribing practice (the "Practice") is
  the responsible party for the information it holds about its patients. KeptCare processes
  patient information only on the Practice's documented instructions, under our Terms and
  Conditions, and for no purpose of our own.

**If you are a patient** of a practice that uses KeptCare, the Practice controls your
information. Please contact your practice first about access, corrections, consent or
deletion. We will assist the Practice in responding to your request.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Practices, users and website visitors (KeptCare is the responsible party)

- **Account information:** name, email address, phone number, practice name, role and team
  membership.
- **Billing information:** your plan, subscription status, and payment history. Card details
  are entered on PayFast's secure pages; KeptCare never receives or stores your full card
  number. We store only the PayFast subscription token needed to bill you.
- **Demo requests:** the name, email, phone number, practice details and message you submit.
- **Technical information:** IP address, browser and device type, and server logs created
  when you use the platform, used for security and troubleshooting.

### 3.2 Patient information (KeptCare is the operator)

Practices upload or capture information about their patients, which may include:

- name, date of birth, gender, phone number and email address;
- visit and appointment history, tags, notes and revenue attributed to the patient;
- communication consent preferences for SMS, email and WhatsApp, and opt-out records;
- messages sent to the patient and their delivery status;
- loyalty points and referral records.

Visit history and practice notes can reveal information about a person's health, which is
**special personal information** under POPIA. Practices are responsible for having a lawful
basis to process it and to send messages to their patients. KeptCare processes it only to
provide the service to the Practice.

## 4. How we use personal information

We use the information in section 3.1 to:

- create and manage accounts and give users secure access;
- provide, maintain, secure and improve the platform;
- process subscriptions, trials and payments;
- send service messages such as account, security and billing notifications;
- respond to support queries and demo requests;
- meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

We use patient information (section 3.2) only to provide the service the Practice has asked
for: storing patient records, displaying analytics, and sending the reminders, campaigns and
other messages the Practice configures, through the channels the patient has consented to.

We do **not** sell personal information, and we do not use patient information for our own
marketing or to build profiles for any third party.

## 5. Lawful basis for processing

We process personal information where it is necessary to perform our contract with you, to
comply with a legal obligation, to protect our legitimate interests or yours (such as
securing the platform and preventing fraud), or with your consent. For patient information,
the Practice determines and is responsible for the lawful basis.

## 6. Patient messaging and direct marketing

- KeptCare records consent separately for each channel (SMS, email and WhatsApp) and will not
  send a patient a message on a channel they have not consented to.
- Patients can opt out at any time: by replying **STOP** to an SMS, using the **unsubscribe**
  link in an email, or by asking their practice. Opting out takes effect for that channel and
  is recorded against the patient's record.
- Practices remain responsible for ensuring that their messages comply with section 69 of
  POPIA (direct marketing by electronic communication) and the Consumer Protection Act.

## 8. Transfers outside South Africa

Some of our service providers process information outside South Africa. Where this happens,
we transfer personal information only as permitted by section 72 of POPIA: to countries or
recipients bound by laws, binding corporate rules or agreements that provide an adequate
level of protection, or where the transfer is necessary to perform our contract with you.

## 9. How we protect personal information

We maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

- encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest;
- strict separation of each practice's data, enforced in the database;
- role-based permissions for practice users;
- restricted, logged access for KeptCare staff;
- verification of incoming webhooks from payment and messaging providers.

If we become aware of a security compromise affecting personal information, we will notify the
Information Regulator and affected parties as required by section 22 of POPIA. Where patient
information is affected, we will notify the Practice within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

## 10. How long we keep information

- **Account and billing records:** for as long as the account is active, and afterwards for
  as long as the law requires (for example, financial records are kept for at least 5 years).
- **Patient information:** for as long as the Practice's subscription is active. When a
  Practice cancels, it can export its data. We delete patient information within
  **[90] days** after the subscription ends, unless the Practice asks us to delete it sooner
  or the law requires us to keep it.
- **Backups:** deleted information may remain in encrypted backups for up to
  **[30] days** before being overwritten.

## 11. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

- ask whether we hold personal information about you, and request a copy of it;
- ask us to correct, update or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date, excessive
  or unlawfully obtained;
- object to processing, including direct marketing, at any time;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To exercise these rights, contact our Information Officer at **[privacy@kept-care.co.za]**.
We may need to verify your identity first. Patients should contact their practice first (see
section 2).

## 12. Cookies and local storage

KeptCare uses only the essential cookies and browser storage needed to keep you signed in and
make the platform work. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## 13. Children

KeptCare is not intended for use by children. Practices may hold information about patients
under 18. Where they do, the Practice is responsible for obtaining the consent of a competent
person (such as a parent or guardian) as required by POPIA.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this
page with a new "Last Updated" date, and notify subscribers of material changes by email.
